# Privacy Policy for ReadThatOut

Last updated: 2026-09-07

ReadThatOut is a Chrome extension that reads web articles aloud on your device. It does not have an account, does not use analytics, and does not sell or share personal data.

## What data is used

**Website content.** When you press Read (or use “Read selection aloud”), the extension reads the article text — or the text you selected — on the current page so it can speak it and highlight the current word. That text stays on your device. It is not uploaded, logged, or sent to us.

**Settings.** Voice, reading speed, highlight color, auto-scroll, and the system-voice fallback preference are stored in Chrome sync so they follow you across devices. Chrome may transmit those settings to Google as part of [Chrome sync](https://support.google.com/chrome/answer/185277).

ReadThatOut does not collect names, email addresses, passwords, location, browsing history, health or financial information, or personal communications.

## How data is stored

- Article text is processed in memory on your device for the current reading session. It is not saved after you stop or close the tab.
- Settings are stored with Chrome’s sync storage.
- The AI voice model and any downloaded voice files are cached in your browser (about 326 MB for the model) so later reading can work offline.

## How data is used

Website content is used only to generate speech and highlight the word being read. Settings are used only to apply your playback preferences. Nothing is used for advertising, profiling, or creditworthiness.

## Network and third parties

The only network request the extension makes is a download of the public AI model and voice files from [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/privacy) (`https://huggingface.co/`). After that download, reading works offline.

ReadThatOut does not send page text, settings, or other user data to Hugging Face, to the developer, or to any other third party. Chrome sync of settings is handled by Google as part of Chrome.

## Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, or transfer user data to third parties. We do not use user data for purposes unrelated to reading articles aloud.

## Retention and deletion

- Cached model and voice files remain until you clear them in **Options → Clear cached model & voices**, or until you uninstall the extension.
- Synced settings remain until you change them, clear Chrome sync data, or uninstall the extension.
- Uninstalling ReadThatOut removes the extension and its local cache.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the “Last updated” date above will be revised. The current version will always be this page.
